French Fries

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Frying Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Directions

  1. Soak your potatoes in cold water
  2. Peal your potatoes until clean
  3. Slice your potatoes into thin long rectangles
  4. Take your pan and pour your oil
  5. Set the stove to Medium High
  6. While waiting for oil to heat up season potatoes with salt and pepper
  7. Fry oil until golden brown
